    Questions

    Questions people ask before they book

    How do I know a hospital is accredited?

    Look for Joint Commission International (JCI) accreditation and ask the hospital for its certificate. Turkey, Thailand and India each have dozens of JCI-accredited hospitals. Accreditation covers the hospital, so also ask about your surgeon's own training and case history.

    What can a quote leave out?

    Follow-up visits, extra hospital nights, translation, travel insurance and treatment for complications are the usual gaps. Ask for a written quote that lists exactly what is included and what is not.

    Can I combine dental and cosmetic treatment in one trip?

    Often, yes. It depends on your health, how long each procedure takes to heal and what your surgeon recommends. Your surgeon should confirm the plan before you book flights.

    Do I need a visa?

    It depends on your passport. As of early 2026, Turkey allows US, UK and most EU citizens to enter visa-free for up to 90 days, while some nationalities need an e-visa. Rules for Thailand, South Korea and India differ, so check the official government site before you book.

    What happens if something goes wrong after I fly home?

    Ask before you book: how long the hospital keeps in touch, whether follow-up appointments can be remote, and who pays if a revision is needed. Get the answers in writing.

    Is Aesthetic Tourism a medical provider?

    No. We help you compare independent hospitals, clinics and surgeons and request quotes. Your treatment, and your medical decisions, are between you and your surgeon.
